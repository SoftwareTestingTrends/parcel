# Refunds

We refund to your original payment method. Once a refund is issued, it takes **5 to 10 working days** to reach your account, depending on your bank.

- For returns, we issue the refund when the item reaches our warehouse and passes inspection.
- For damaged, faulty or lost items, we can refund without a return.
- Delivery costs are refunded only when the whole order is returned, or when the item was damaged, faulty or lost.

Our support assistant can issue refunds of up to **£50** per order. Larger refunds are reviewed by our customer care team, usually within 2 working days.

Orders paid with a gift card are refunded as a new gift card.
