# Returning an item

You can return most items within **30 days of delivery** for a refund, as long as they're unused and in their original packaging.

To start a return, go to **My orders**, choose the item and select **Return**. We'll email you a prepaid returns label. Returns are free for UK addresses; returns from Ireland and the EU cost £6.95, taken from your refund.

Large items are collected by courier. Book a collection from **My orders**.

Some items can't be returned. See "Items we can't take back".
