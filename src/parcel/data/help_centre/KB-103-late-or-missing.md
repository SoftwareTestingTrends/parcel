# Late or missing parcels

If your parcel is more than 3 working days late, or the tracking says delivered but you can't find it:

1. Check with neighbours and any safe place you gave the courier.
2. Wait one more working day, as couriers sometimes mark parcels delivered early.
3. Contact us with your order number.

If a parcel is confirmed lost, we'll send a replacement or give a full refund, including delivery costs. We investigate with the courier first, which can take up to 5 working days.
