# Delivery options and costs

We deliver to addresses in the United Kingdom, Ireland and selected EU countries.

- **Standard delivery** takes 3 to 5 working days and costs £3.95. It's free on orders over £40.
- **Express delivery** takes 1 to 2 working days and costs £7.95. Order before 2pm for dispatch the same working day.
- **Large items** (furniture, rugs over 2 metres) go by two-person courier. They take 5 to 10 working days and cost £29.

Working days are Monday to Friday, not counting UK bank holidays. Delivery times start from dispatch, not from the moment you order.
