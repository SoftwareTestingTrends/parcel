# Tracking your order

When your order is dispatched we email you a tracking link. You can also see your order's status in your Hollow Lane account under **My orders**.

Order statuses mean:

- **Processing**: we've received your order and are packing it. You can still cancel.
- **Dispatched**: it's with the courier. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear.
- **Delivered**: the courier has marked it as delivered.

If your tracking hasn't changed for 3 working days, see "Late or missing parcels".
