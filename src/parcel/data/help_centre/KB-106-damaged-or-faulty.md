# Damaged or faulty items

If something arrives damaged or develops a fault within 12 months, we'll put it right.

Please contact us within **14 days of delivery** for damage in transit, with your order number and a photo of the damage and the packaging.

Depending on the item, we'll offer a replacement, a repair or a refund. You don't need to send small damaged items back. For large items we'll arrange a collection.

If a faulty item has caused an injury, a fire or any other safety problem, stop using it and ask to speak to our customer care team straight away.
